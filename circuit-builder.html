How to use this

  1. 1Pick a board: Series, Parallel or Combo.
  2. 2Drag a part onto a dashed slot. Or tap the part, then tap the slot.
  3. 3Tap a part on the board to remove it.
  4. 4Tap a slot’s closed tag to open its switch.
  5. 5Watch the 3D view. Height is voltage. Dot flow is current. A narrow channel is resistance.
