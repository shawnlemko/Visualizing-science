How to use this

  1. 1Press Start. The pink nutrient moves into every cell at the same speed.
  2. 2It stops when cell A is full. Read the bars: B and C are not full yet.
  3. 3Press Start again. Watch which nucleus turns red (starving) and which turns green (fed).
  4. 4Drag the Cell C size slider and race again.
  5. 5Press More, then Lock V at 64 cm³. Change C's shape. Same volume, more surface area, fed sooner.
  1. 1Drag Membrane folding. The surface area grows, the volume stays the same.
  2. 2Drag ΔC. A bigger difference in O₂ moves more O₂ across.
  3. 3Drag Membrane thickness. A thicker membrane slows diffusion.
  4. 4Try Pulmonary edema and Emphysema. Compare the rate with healthy.
  5. 5In the math, T is membrane thickness and SAflat is a flat 1 cm² patch of membrane.
