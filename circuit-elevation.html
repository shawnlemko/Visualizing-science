How to use this

  1. 1Pick a circuit.
  2. 2Slide V. The battery cliff gets taller. Height is voltage.
  3. 3Slide R. The channel narrows and the dots slow down. Dot flow is current.
  4. 4Hover any wire to read its voltage and current.
  5. 5In Parallel, switch branch 2 on and off. Watch the current leaving the battery.
