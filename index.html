Visualizing Science Concepts

Interactive visuals for the science classroom. Pick a concept to open it.

  • Matter: Solubility and Precipitates Mix two solutions and see which ions form a solid. 10-12
  • Energy: Heating Curve of Water Add energy to ice and see when to use Q = mcΔt and when to use Q = nH. 10-12
  • Energy: Energy Stores and Pathways Watch energy drain from one store and fill others, while the total stays the same. 7-910-12
  • Energy: Voltage and Current Elevation See voltage as height and current as flow, in series and parallel circuits. 7-910-12
  • Matter: Atoms and Bonding Build Bohr atoms, watch electrons transfer or share, then morph to Lewis dots and criss-cross formulas. 7-910-12
  • Matter: Ionic Formula Builder Balance ion charges to write formulas, with brackets and Roman numerals. 10-12
  • Scientific Methods: Unit Analysis Cancel units to convert kJ to J, km per hour to m per second, and grams to moles. 7-910-12
  • Living Systems: Cell Size Diffusion Race Race nutrients into small and large cells to see why a falling SA : V ratio starves the core. 10-12
  • Energy: Forces in Water and Air Sink or float a block, and fly a wing: buoyant force, lift, weight, thrust and drag. K-67-9
  • Computer Science: Rover Algorithms and AI Loops and if–else blocks, a linear vs binary search race, AI training bias, and the same code in Python. K-67-9
