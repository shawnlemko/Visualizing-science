How to use this

  1. 1Set the mass. The moles appear in purple, because the flat parts need n, not m.
  2. 2Set the start and end temperatures.
  3. 3Press Play or drag the Q slider to add energy.
  4. 4Watch the meters: only Ek or Ep rises at a time.
  5. 5Follow the cards on the right. Orange parts use Q = mcΔt, purple parts use Q = nH. Add the cards for the total.
